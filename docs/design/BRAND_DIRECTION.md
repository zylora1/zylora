# Zylora brand direction

Status: **Phase 0 art direction frozen; visual implementation begins in Phase 3**

## Central idea: the digital atelier

Zylora should feel like a precise editorial workshop where strong starting designs are shaped into a
real business Website. It is not a magical blank prompt, a developer console, or a playful AI toy.
The visual character combines editorial confidence, craft, and operational clarity.

Three attributes guide every surface:

- **Composed:** authored grids, decisive type hierarchy, useful whitespace, no decorative clutter.
- **Tangible:** real Template/editor/publish/Lead UI, paper/ink warmth, clear actions and evidence.
- **Trustworthy:** restrained motion, exact language, visible state, transparent eligibility and price.

## Signature visual language

The foundation is warm limestone and paper against deep botanical ink. A burnt vermilion action color
marks the next consequential step; muted teal supports informational/product state. Fine rules,
editorial crops, asymmetric but disciplined grids, and occasional serif display type make the public
brand recognizable without gradients, glass, glow, or decorative 3D forms.

Application UI becomes denser and quieter: mostly neutral surfaces, sans-serif type, square-to-modest
radii, meaningful status color, strong table/list rhythm, and one clear primary action per region.

## Typography

- Display/editorial: **Newsreader** (self-hosted only after license and subset verification), with
  Georgia as fallback.
- Interface/body: **Manrope** (self-hosted after license verification), with system sans fallbacks.
- Code/identifiers where needed: system monospace; never as decoration.

Marketing may use the serif for high-impact display phrases and section introductions. Product UI uses
sans-serif almost exclusively. Comfortable body measure is 55–72 characters. Numerals align in
tables, prices, analytics, and ledgers.

## Color character

- Botanical ink communicates brand/structure, not generic black.
- Warm canvas reduces sterile dashboard feeling while paper surfaces preserve contrast.
- Vermilion is scarce: primary CTA, selected critical emphasis, and key conversion marker.
- Teal is informational/interactive support, not a competing primary CTA.
- Semantic colors never rely on hue alone and never reuse brand color ambiguously.

Exact tokens and tested combinations live in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

## Imagery and product visuals

Prefer authentic Zylora outputs: full or cropped Template previews, editor detail, publish evaluation,
domain state, Lead records, and responsive device comparisons. Screens should contain deliberate demo
content clearly presented as product preview—not fabricated customer data.

Template photography must be licensed/open/provenanced, industry-appropriate, naturally art-directed,
and responsive. Avoid generic AI people, random abstract blobs, fake logos, stock handshake tropes,
and imagery used merely to fill cards.

## Iconography and illustration

Use one coherent outline icon family with consistent stroke, optical size, and accessible labels.
Icons support recognition; they do not replace text for unfamiliar actions. Custom diagrams use thin
rules, labeled stages, and product-native shapes. Emojis are not navigation or status icons.

## Voice and copy

Voice is concrete, concise, calm, and candid. Explain what happens and what the User controls.

Good:

- “Choose a Template, customize it, then publish on your domain.”
- “This Website has 7 pages. This plan supports up to 5.”
- “Your previous live version is still online. Fix the build issue and try again.”

Avoid inflated promises, generic AI clichés, unexplained jargon, false urgency, fake social proof,
invented testimonials/counts, and vague “Something went wrong” messages.

## Brand acceptance questions

Before approval ask:

1. Could this composition belong unchanged to any generic AI SaaS?
2. Does real Zylora workflow carry the story, or decorative UI?
3. Is the primary action unmistakable without excessive color/pills/cards?
4. Does typography provide identity and hierarchy at every viewport?
5. Are trust, eligibility, price, state, and recovery communicated honestly?

Any “yes” to the first or “no” to the rest requires composition/root-system revision, not recoloring.
