# Landing page art direction

Status: **Narrative frozen; final copy/visuals require implemented product evidence**

## Visitor understanding target

Within the first viewport a visitor should understand: choose an existing professional Template;
customize manually or with AI; publish on Zylora or a custom domain; alternatively transfer or buy a
ZIP; live Websites can capture Leads.

## Composition

1. **Editorial navigation:** logo, Templates, Features, Pricing, Blog; Login and one clear “Choose a
   Template” action. No crowded pill bar.
2. **Hero as decision:** an assertive serif-led statement paired with a real, edge-cropped Template
   browser and a visible Desktop/Tablet/Mobile switch. Supporting copy names publish/transfer/export.
3. **Template index strip:** a browsable slice organized like a design catalog—category labels,
   meaningful preview variation, and real filtering—not three generic feature cards.
4. **Choose → shape → ship:** one continuous horizontal/stacked workflow using actual gallery, editor,
   and publish evaluation states. Manual ↔ AI is shown as two inputs changing the same document.
5. **Destination ledger:** a precise comparison of Zylora subdomain, custom domain, Transfer, and paid
   ZIP with ownership and payment facts.
6. **Lead loop:** a Website page transitions into chatbot/form Lead, one credit record, notification,
   and real Portal row. This is a causal diagram/product sequence, not a fake dashboard collage.
7. **Four-plan pricing:** read current backend catalog, show exact four plans and capability language;
   no hard-coded or invented price.
8. **Buying questions/FAQ:** ownership, domains, editing constraints, transfers, ZIP contents, Leads,
   billing and cancellation.
9. **Final Template CTA and SEO footer:** concrete action, complete public/legal navigation.

## Visual rhythm

Alternate warm canvas, paper, and occasional botanical-ink bands. Use full-width rules, offset type,
cropped product panels, and intentional overlap only where it explains continuity. Avoid repetitive
centered headings followed by three cards. Sections earn their height through information, not empty
“premium” whitespace.

## Motion and interaction

Motion demonstrates causality: device preview changes, a structured edit updates one component,
publish stages advance, and a Lead appears in the ledger. It never fakes activity or metrics. Scroll
reveal is restrained; no scroll hijack, giant parallax, glow, or autoplay motion that blocks reading.
All demonstrations pause, work with keyboard/touch, and respect reduced motion.

## Content rules

- Product screenshots use implemented UI and clearly labeled demo Websites.
- No customer logos, testimonials, counts, ratings, awards, or revenue without verified data.
- No claims of instant generation from a blank prompt.
- Copy names limitations honestly: Template-first editing, current plan requirements, paid ZIP.
- Template imagery has license/provenance and meaningful category fit.

## Conversion and SEO

Primary CTA is “Choose a Template”; secondary is “See how publishing works.” Page uses one semantic
H1, descriptive headings, useful internal links, crawlable content, structured data appropriate to
actual product/FAQ, responsive optimized images, reserved media dimensions, and minimal client JS.
Portal/admin content never leaks into indexable markup.

## Review criteria

Review at all required widths for instant comprehension, CTA hierarchy, actual buying questions,
typographic authorship, real product evidence, content order, keyboard/reduced motion, and performance.
If the page could be summarized as hero + cards + testimonials + pricing, redesign the narrative.
